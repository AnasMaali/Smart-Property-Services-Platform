# Super Admin / Company Owner

## Role Name

**Super Admin / Company Owner**

## Arabic Name

**السوبر أدمن / صاحب الشركة**

---

## Role Status

**Future Role — Not Included in Version 1**

---

## Role Description

The Super Admin / Company Owner is the highest-level user in the system.

This role represents the business owner or the main system manager who has full control over the platform, users, settings, permissions, reports, subscriptions, and business analytics.

In Version 1, this role will not be implemented as a separate role. The system will start with the Admin / Service Management Team to manage the basic booking and service flow.

The Super Admin role can be added in future versions when the system becomes larger and needs advanced permission control, business monitoring, and management features.

---

## Main Purpose

The main purpose of this role is to give the company owner or main manager full control over the system and business operations.

This role helps separate high-level business decisions from daily service management tasks.

---

## Future Responsibilities

In future versions, the Super Admin / Company Owner may be responsible for:

* Managing admin accounts.
* Adding, editing, or removing admins.
* Managing user roles and permissions.
* Managing system settings.
* Viewing all customer booking requests.
* Viewing all technicians and employees.
* Managing services and service categories.
* Managing subscription plans in future versions.
* Viewing business reports.
* Viewing financial summaries.
* Monitoring customer satisfaction.
* Monitoring service performance.
* Accessing AI-based data analysis reports.
* Reviewing emergency service statistics.
* Managing cancellation and refund policies.
* Controlling platform-level settings.

---

## Difference Between Admin and Super Admin

The Admin / Service Management Team focuses on daily service operations, such as managing paid booking requests, assigning technicians, updating request statuses, and handling customer support.

The Super Admin / Company Owner focuses on higher-level system and business management, such as managing admins, permissions, reports, subscriptions, system settings, and business analytics.

---

## Possible Future Functions

The Super Admin may be able to:

* Log in to the super admin dashboard.
* Create admin accounts.
* Edit admin accounts.
* Disable admin accounts.
* Manage role permissions.
* View all system activities.
* View all customers.
* View all service requests.
* View all payments.
* View all ratings and feedback.
* View reports and analytics.
* Manage platform settings.
* Manage annual subscription plans.
* Access AI data analysis results.
* Export reports.
* Monitor overall system performance.

---

## Version 1 Decision

This role will not be implemented in Version 1.

In Version 1, the Admin / Service Management Team will handle the basic management needs of the system.

The Super Admin role will be revisited in future versions when the system needs advanced permissions, business reports, subscription management, and AI analytics dashboards.

---

## Notes

* This is a future role.
* It is not required for the first version.
* It will be useful when the system grows.
* It will help separate daily operations from high-level business control.
* It can manage admins, permissions, reports, subscriptions, and business analytics in future versions.
