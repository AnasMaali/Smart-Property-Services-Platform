# Subscription Customer / Annual Contract Customer

## Role Name

**Subscription Customer / Annual Contract Customer**

## Arabic Name

**العميل المشترك / عميل الاشتراك السنوي**

---

## Role Status

**Future Role — Not Included in Version 1**

This role is planned for future versions of the system and will not be included in the first version.

In Version 1, the system will focus mainly on the **Customer / One-Time Customer**, who requests and pays for individual services without an annual subscription.

---

## Role Description

The Subscription Customer / Annual Contract Customer is a future type of customer who will have an annual subscription, service package, or long-term contract with the service company.

Unlike the One-Time Customer, this user will not only request a single service when needed. Instead, the user may have an ongoing relationship with the company through a yearly plan or contract.

This role may be useful for customers who own or manage properties that need repeated or continuous services, such as villas, buildings, offices, companies, or rental properties.

---

## Main Purpose

The main purpose of this future role is to support customers who need regular property services through an organized subscription or contract-based model.

This can help the company provide continuous services, scheduled maintenance, priority support, and better long-term customer management.

---

## Possible Future Use Cases

This role may be useful for:

* A villa owner who wants yearly maintenance support.
* A building owner who needs regular cleaning and maintenance.
* An office manager who needs continuous property services.
* A company that wants a long-term service contract.
* A customer who wants priority support for emergency cases.

---

## Possible Future Functions

The following functions may be added in future versions:

* Create an account.
* Verify the phone number using OTP.
* Log in to the system.
* Edit personal profile information.
* Subscribe to an annual plan or service package.
* View subscription details.
* Renew the annual subscription.
* Add and manage one or more properties.
* View services included in the subscription.
* Request services covered by the subscription.
* Request emergency services based on the subscription plan.
* Track service requests.
* View used and remaining services, if the plan has limits.
* Pay annual subscription fees.
* Pay extra fees for services not included in the subscription.
* Contact human support.
* Use the AI chatbot in future versions.
* Rate completed services.

---

## Future Planning Notes

This role needs more detailed planning before implementation.

In future versions, the team should decide:

* Whether subscriptions will be fixed packages or custom contracts.
* What services will be included in each subscription.
* Whether emergency services are included or charged separately.
* How many properties each customer can add.
* Whether the customer can add multiple users under one subscription.
* How renewal, cancellation, and payment will work.
* Whether subscription customers get priority over one-time customers.

---

## Version 1 Decision

This role will not be implemented in Version 1.

Version 1 will focus on the basic service booking process for one-time customers.

The Subscription Customer role will be revisited after the core booking system, admin management, payment flow, and service request tracking are completed.
