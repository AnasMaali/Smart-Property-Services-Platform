# AI Services / AI Models

## Role Name

**AI Services / AI Models**

## Arabic Name

**خدمات الذكاء الاصطناعي / نماذج الذكاء الاصطناعي**

---

## Role Type

**System Component / Non-Human Stakeholder**

---

## Role Status

**Future Component — Not Included in Version 1**

---

## Description

AI Services represent the artificial intelligence components that will be added to the Smart Property Services Platform in future versions.

These services are not human users. They are intelligent system components that support customers, improve service quality, and help the company make better decisions based on data.

In Version 1, the system will focus on the core booking flow, service management, payment, and admin operations. The AI services will be planned and added later after the basic system becomes stable.

---

## Main Purpose

The main purpose of AI Services is to make the platform smarter, faster, and more helpful for both customers and the company.

AI can support the system by answering user questions, analyzing company data, and helping classify property problems from uploaded images.

---

## Planned AI Models

The system will include three main AI models in future versions:

---

## 1. AI Chatbot

The AI Chatbot will provide quick support to users inside the website or mobile application.

It may help users with:

* Answering questions about the platform.
* Explaining available services.
* Guiding users through the booking process.
* Providing general company or service information.
* Reducing simple support requests handled by human support.
* Helping users understand what service they may need.

In Version 1, support can be handled by human support or basic contact options. The chatbot can be added later.

---

## 2. Data Analysis Model

The Data Analysis Model will analyze company and platform data to help improve business decisions.

It may analyze:

* Number of service requests.
* Most requested services.
* Emergency request frequency.
* Customer ratings.
* Service completion time.
* Technician performance.
* Busy days and times.
* Customer behavior.
* Revenue and payment trends.

This model can help the company improve operations, service quality, customer satisfaction, and future planning.

---

## 3. Image Analysis Model

The Image Analysis Model will allow customers to upload a picture of a property problem.

The model may analyze the image to:

* Identify the possible problem or damage.
* Suggest the most suitable service category.
* Help the customer choose the right service.
* Help the admin understand the issue faster.
* Support emergency or maintenance request classification.

Examples may include:

* Water leakage.
* Wall damage.
* Electrical issue.
* Broken item.
* Mold or ceiling damage.
* Maintenance problem.

This feature will be added later when the AI image model is ready.

---

## Version 1 Decision

AI Services will not be implemented in Version 1.

Version 1 will focus on:

* Customer account creation.
* OTP verification.
* Service selection.
* Booking available date and time.
* Property information during booking.
* Payment.
* Admin request management.
* Technician assignment by admin.
* Request status tracking.
* Human support.
* Service rating.

AI features will be added in later versions after the core system is completed.

---

## Future Integration Notes

In future versions, AI Services should be connected to the main system through APIs.

The recommended structure is:

**Website / Mobile App → Backend → AI Service → Backend → User or Admin**

This keeps the AI section separate from the core system and makes it easier to develop, test, and improve each AI model independently.

---

## Notes

* AI Services are not human users.
* AI Services are future system components.
* They are not required for Version 1.
* The AI section should stay separate from the core system.
* The three planned AI models are Chatbot, Data Analysis, and Image Analysis.
* AI features can be added step by step after the main platform is working.
