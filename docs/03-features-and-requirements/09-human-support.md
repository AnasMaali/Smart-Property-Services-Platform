# Human Support

## Purpose

This document defines how the Customer / One-Time Customer can contact human support when assistance is needed.

---

## Version 1 Scope

In Version 1, human support is available to help customers with booking, payment, service details, account issues, and request follow-up.

The AI chatbot is not included in Version 1.

---

## Customer Support Access

The customer should be able to contact human support through a clear support option inside the mobile application.

Possible support methods may include:

* Phone call
* WhatsApp
* Support form
* In-app support message
* Email

The final support channels can be selected based on the company’s operational needs.

---

## Support Cases

The customer may contact human support for cases such as:

* Problems creating or accessing an account
* OTP verification issues
* Questions about available services
* Problems with the cart
* Payment problems
* Incorrect booking information
* Questions about request status
* Technician or service concerns
* Cancellation requests
* General complaints or feedback

---

## Support Request Information

When submitting a support request, the customer may provide:

* Name
* Phone number
* Booking ID, if related to a booking
* Support category
* Message or problem description
* Contact preference

---

## Admin Responsibilities

The Admin / Service Management Team should be able to:

* View customer support requests.
* Review the customer’s message.
* View related booking details if available.
* Contact the customer.
* Update the support request status.
* Close the support request after resolving the issue.

---

## Support Request Statuses

Possible support request statuses may include:

* Open
* In Progress
* Resolved
* Closed

---

## Important Rules

* Human support is included in Version 1.
* Support should be easy to access from the mobile application.
* The customer should not need to create a new account to contact support for login or OTP problems.
* Support requests related to bookings should be connected to the relevant Booking ID when possible.
* The AI chatbot may be added in a future version.
