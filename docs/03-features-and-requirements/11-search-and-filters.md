# Search & Filters

## Purpose

This feature allows the customer to find services quickly inside the mobile application.

---

## Version 1 Scope

The customer can:

* Search for a service by name.
* Browse services by category.
* Filter services based on their category.
* Clear the search or selected filters.

---

## Possible Categories

Possible service categories may include:

* Cleaning
* AC
* Plumbing
* Electrical
* Painting
* Handyman
* Other

---

## Important Rules

* Search results should display only active services.
* Services and categories are managed by the Admin.
* The customer can open a service from the search results.
* The customer can select the service details and add it to the cart.
* Search and filter results should update based on the customer’s selected category or search text.
