# Property Information During Booking

## Purpose

This document defines the property information that the Customer / One-Time Customer must enter during the booking process.

In Version 1, the customer does not add or manage saved properties in advance. Property information is entered only when the customer is creating a booking.

---

## Version 1 Scope

In Version 1, this module is designed for the **Customer / One-Time Customer** only.

The customer enters property information during the cart-based booking process.

The same property information applies to all services inside the cart.

---

## Main Rule

The customer should not be required to add property details during account registration.

Property details should be collected only during the booking process.

This keeps registration simple and makes the booking process more accurate because the customer enters the property information related to the current service request.

---

## Required Property Information

During booking, the customer should enter the following property information:

* Property type
* City
* Area
* Full address
* Floor number, if needed
* Apartment or unit number, if needed
* Additional location notes
* Contact number for the visit

---

## Property Type Options

The property type may include:

* Apartment
* House
* Villa
* Building
* Office
* Other

---

## Address Details

The customer should enter enough address information to help the company and technician reach the property.

Address details may include:

* City
* Area
* Street name
* Building name or number
* Floor number
* Apartment or unit number
* Nearby landmark
* Additional notes

---

## Contact Number for Visit

The customer should provide a contact number for the visit.

This number can be the same as the account phone number or a different number if another person will be available at the property.

---

## Location Notes

The customer can add extra location notes to help the technician find the property.

Examples:

* Near the main mosque
* Behind the supermarket
* Second building after the traffic light
* Use the side entrance
* Call before arrival

---

## Cart-Based Booking Rule

If the customer adds multiple services to the cart, all services inside the cart must be for the same property.

This means the customer enters the property information once, and it applies to all selected services in the cart.

Example:

Selected services:

* AC Cleaning
* Sofa Cleaning
* Curtain Installation

Property information:

* Apartment
* Ramallah
* Al-Masyoun
* Building 12
* Floor 3
* Apartment 7

All selected services will be performed at the same property.

---

## Not Included in Version 1

The following property-related features are not included in Version 1:

* Saved property management
* Multiple saved properties
* Property dashboard
* Property maintenance history
* Property images
* Property invoices
* Property contracts
* Asset management
* Home health score
* Map-based location selection

These features can be planned for future versions.

---

## Version 1 Booking Flow Placement

Property information is entered after the customer adds services to the cart and before choosing or confirming the final booking details.

The flow is:

1. Customer chooses one or more regular services.
2. Customer adds services to the cart.
3. Customer enters property information.
4. Customer chooses one available date and time for the whole cart.
5. Customer reviews the cart and property information.
6. Customer confirms the booking.
7. Customer pays once for the total cart amount.

---

## Notes

* Property information is entered during booking only.
* The customer does not manage saved properties in Version 1.
* The same property information applies to all services inside the cart.
* Property information is required before payment.
* The admin can view the property information after successful payment.
* The technician uses the property information to reach the service location.
