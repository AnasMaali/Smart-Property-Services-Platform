12. Booking History & Rebooking
Purpose

This feature allows the customer to view current and previous booking requests and repeat a previous booking when needed.

Booking History

The customer should be able to view:

Current bookings
Previous bookings
Booking ID
Selected services
Property information
Booking date and time
Total price
Payment status
Booking status
Rebook Service

Completed bookings should include a:

Book Again

button.

When the customer selects Book Again:

The previous services are added to a new cart.
The previous service details are copied when possible.
The customer must review the cart.
The customer must enter or confirm the property information.
The customer must choose a new available date and time.
The current service prices should be used.
The customer must complete a new payment.
Important Rules
Rebooking creates a new booking request.
The previous booking is not modified.
Services that are no longer available should not be added automatically.
The customer must confirm all details before payment.
