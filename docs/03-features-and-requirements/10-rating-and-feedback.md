# Rating & Feedback

## Purpose

This document defines how the Customer / One-Time Customer can rate the completed service and provide feedback.

---

## Version 1 Scope

In Version 1, the customer can rate the service only after the booking is marked as Completed.

The rating helps the company understand customer satisfaction and improve service quality.

---

## Customer Rating

After the service is completed, the customer should be able to:

* Give a star rating.
* Write an optional comment.
* Rate the overall service experience.
* Submit feedback about the completed booking.

A simple rating scale may be:

* 1 Star — Very Poor
* 2 Stars — Poor
* 3 Stars — Average
* 4 Stars — Good
* 5 Stars — Excellent

---

## Multi-Service Booking Rule

A booking may contain one service or multiple services.

In Version 1, the customer can provide one overall rating for the full booking.

Rating each service or technician separately can be added in a future version.

---

## Rating Flow

1. The booking status becomes Completed.
2. The customer opens the completed booking.
3. The system shows the rating option.
4. The customer selects a star rating.
5. The customer may write an optional comment.
6. The customer submits the rating.
7. The rating is stored and becomes available to the Admin / Service Management Team.

---

## Admin View

The Admin / Service Management Team should be able to:

* View customer ratings.
* View customer comments.
* View the booking related to the rating.
* Review low ratings.
* Use feedback to improve service quality.

---

## Important Rules

* The customer can rate only completed bookings.
* The customer can submit one rating per booking.
* The written comment is optional.
* The rating should be connected to the related Booking ID.
* The customer should not be able to rate the same booking more than once.
* Editing or deleting a submitted rating can be considered in a future version.

---

## Future Improvements

Future versions may include:

* Rating each service separately.
* Rating each technician separately.
* Uploading images with feedback.
* Admin responses to customer feedback.
* Automatic follow-up for low ratings.
* AI-based sentiment analysis.
* More detailed satisfaction questions.
