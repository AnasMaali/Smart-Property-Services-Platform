## Step 1: Product Discovery & System Vision

The proposed system is a digital property services platform designed to help property owners request, book, and manage property-related services through a website and mobile application.

The system targets people who own or manage properties such as apartments, independent houses, villas, buildings, offices, and other real estate units. These users often need maintenance, repair, renovation, cleaning, inspection, or emergency services, but they may face difficulty finding reliable service providers quickly, safely, and in an organized way.

The main problem solved by the system is the lack of a trusted and organized digital process for requesting property services. Instead of manually searching for a technician, worker, or service provider, the user can access the platform, select the needed service, describe the problem, choose or request a suitable time, and submit the booking directly through the system.

After the request is submitted, the responsible management team receives the booking, reviews the details, manages the request, and assigns the suitable technician or employee to visit the property and provide the required service.

The core value of the system is to save time and effort for property owners, reduce the stress of finding trusted service providers, improve response speed in normal and emergency cases, and help the service company organize its operations in a more professional and digital way.

The platform will be available as both a website and a mobile application for iOS and Android, allowing users to request services easily from different devices.

The system will also include three artificial intelligence models. The first model is a chatbot that provides quick support to users, answers questions about the company and services, and guides users through the booking process. The second model is a data analysis system that analyzes company data to help improve operations, service quality, customer satisfaction, and business decisions. The third model is an image analysis system that allows users to upload a picture of a property problem, then analyzes the image to identify the possible issue or damage and suggest the most suitable service category.
